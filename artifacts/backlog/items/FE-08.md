## What

`standards init` must not classify a repository containing shipped work as `greenfield`. That is
proposal 0004's release objective, unchanged, and this feature is the whole of what its `build`
decision authorized.

## Why

A false `greenfield` suppresses the `undocumented-decisions` warning — the one output that tells an
adopter with shipped work not to back-fill proposals for it. The module's own comment names this as
the dangerous direction, and the first repository ever to adopt these standards was misclassified
this way on its first run.

## Notes

**This is the first backlog item created from a proposal decision.** It carries the authorizing
proposal in its `evidence` because it is the highest item whose entire subtree 0004 authorizes; its
parent epic covers more than 0004 does, and its three stories are all inside the authorization. Per
[SCOPE.md](../SCOPE.md), none of those stories repeats the proposal path — one statement of the fact,
in one place, so the copies cannot disagree.

### The constraint a reviewer can enforce

**0004 may change how implementation evidence prevents `GREENFIELD`. It may not reinterpret prompt
artifacts as innovation proposals, and it may not choose among the remap / drop / report alternatives
owned by [proposal 0006](../../innovation-proposals/0006-prompt-markers-are-not-recorded-decisions.md).**

That constraint has teeth because the two defects meet in one function. `PROMPT_MARKERS` currently
routes to `EXISTING_WITH_PROPOSALS`, and the experiment showed that under bounded recursion a real
repository moves from `greenfield` into that branch rather than into the correct mode. **Leave that
branch visibly wrong.** A repository that lands in `existing-with-proposals` after this work remains
0006's defect, unchanged and still observable — which is what keeps 0006 a decision someone makes
rather than a question answered silently inside another proposal's implementation.

### What authorization did not settle

The composition that classified all six experiment subjects correctly is recorded in 0004 as E14, a
**hypothesis**. It was derived after the run, and one of those six subjects was built after the run
to falsify its predecessor. ST-01 exists because implementing from it without pre-registering it
would convert exploratory optimization into confirmatory evidence.
