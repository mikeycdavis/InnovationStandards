## What

The content-agnostic machinery copied from EngineeringStandards, the numbered source spec and its
inventory authored **before the first standard existed**, and canonical rule identity fixed on day
one as `innovation.<kebab-case>` with every `aliases` array empty.

## Why

Checks before content. A gate erected after the documents it governs is a gate shaped by them —
it passes because the content already matches, not because the content was held to anything.

## Notes

Fixing rule identity first was the source framework's most expensive lesson, inherited rather than
relearned: it had drifted into speaking finding categories in the audit while the policy spoke rule
ids, and the alias mechanism exists there to paper over it. Here `aliases` stays `[]` through 1.x.

The vendored comments came with rationale that referred to standards this repository does not have.
That was not visible at the time — it surfaced months later and took two dedicated sweeps to
correct. See the post-v1.0.0 commits `d7c9e73` and `3ee1985`. Tests establish that copied *behaviour*
works; nothing established that copied *comments* still explained why this repository needed it.
