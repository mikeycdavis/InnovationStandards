## What

A fifth verdict, `BLOCKED_BY_INVARIANT`, added to the compliance engine; every engineering
detector removed and replaced with thirteen proposal detectors over a single parse pass; two new
read-only subcommands, `check` and `explain`; and the repository's first two real proposals
written the moment the CLI could evaluate them.

## Why

The fifth status is the signal an AI agent must stop on. Without it, an attempt to fabricate
evidence and an ordinary missing section produce the same word, and "non-compliant" is a state
agents are trained to work around.

## Notes

`BLOCKED_BY_INVARIANT` strictly strengthens: it can only replace what would otherwise have been
`NON_COMPLIANT`, never a passing status. That is asserted in the tests, because a new status that
could displace a pass would be a weakening dressed as a strengthening.

Proposal 0002 is the load-bearing one. It records a **rejected** alternative — sharing the scripts
as an npm package — and it passes the evaluator identically to a `build`. That is the mechanical
guarantee that no idea is forced toward implementation, and "do not build this" is a compliant
conclusion rather than a process failure.

Detectors only ever parse `artifacts/innovation-proposals/*.md`. Prose describing the taxonomy
anywhere else is invisible to them — the mentions-versus-uses guard, which this backlog's own
documents now exercise for real.
