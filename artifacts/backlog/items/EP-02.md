## What

The defects in `scripts/init.mjs` that the first independent adoption surfaced, and the work
authorized to correct them.

## Why

Three separate proposals — 0004, 0005 and 0006 — each name a different defect in the same script.
Grouping them under one epic keeps the implementation work discoverable without merging the
decisions, which are deliberately separate documents with separate outcomes.

## Notes

**This epic is not wholly authorized by any one proposal, and that is why it carries no proposal in
its `evidence`.** Only [FE-08](FE-08.md) beneath it is authorized, and by 0004 alone. 0005 and 0006
are both `explore`; neither authorizes implementation, so neither has work here. If either later
reaches `build`, it produces its own feature under this epic carrying its own authorization.

The provenance rule this follows is recorded in [SCOPE.md](../SCOPE.md): the highest item whose
entire subtree is authorized by a proposal names it, and nothing below repeats it. This epic is one
level above that line.
