## What

Freeze the derived candidate — bounded recursion composed with the no-early-return rule — and its
expected classification for every subject, in writing, **before** running it again. Then re-run
`artifacts/experiments/0004-mode-detection/harness.mjs` and record what came back.

## Why

The candidate's 6/6 is not a confirmatory result. It was assembled after seeing which pre-registered
candidate won, and one of the six subjects was constructed after that too, specifically to break the
winner. A result derived from the data it is then tested against establishes nothing, and
authorization to implement does not retroactively change how that number was produced.

## Notes

The point of ordering this first is that pre-registration is only meaningful before the run. Doing it
after the implementation exists would be writing down a prediction whose answer is already sitting in
the working tree.

Expected classifications must be recorded per subject, not as a score. "6/6" is compatible with being
right for the wrong reason on two of them.

If the re-run disagrees with the frozen expectation, that is the useful outcome and it belongs back
in proposal 0004 as evidence — not quietly reconciled here. The proposal remains the authoritative
record of what the evidence says; this backlog only records that the work happened.
