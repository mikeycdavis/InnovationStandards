## What

Fixtures and tests covering both directions of the correction: the false-greenfield reproduction that
started this, and the case that exposed the weakness of root-only detection.

## Why

0004's success criteria are written as observations, and each names a test that must exist. Without
them the correction is asserted rather than demonstrated, and the next change to `detectMode` has
nothing to fail against.

## Notes

At minimum two cases, and they are not interchangeable:

- **The HouseDoc shape** — implementation markers one level below the root, no proposals. This is the
  observed misclassification, reduced to a fixture. It must be shown failing against the current
  implementation first; a regression test that has never been red is a test of nothing.
- **The bare monorepo** — markers one level down, no `artifacts/` at all. This one exposed that
  root-only signal combination still reads greenfield.

**Label the second one as what it is.** It was constructed after the experiment, to falsify the
candidate that had just won. In the fixture, in its comment, and anywhere it is described, it is a
post-hoc falsifier — not pre-registered evidence. Re-describing it as part of the original experiment
would make the record say the design was confirmed by a test it was actually shaped around.

The empty-directory case must stay green throughout, or the fix is a fix in one direction and the
`greenfield` mode becomes unreachable.
