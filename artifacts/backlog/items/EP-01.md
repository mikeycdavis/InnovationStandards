## What

The complete first release: 14 standards, 29 rules, an adapted evaluator with a fifth verdict,
a five-command CLI, a mutation-tested suite, and documentation that publishes its own coverage
limits. Tagged `v1.0.0`.

## Notes

**The seven features beneath this epic did not each get a commit.** The entire baseline landed as
a single commit, `a75d532`, so every child cites that SHA plus the paths it produced. The
milestones were a plan structure and a review structure; they were never a commit structure, and
recording them as if they had been would be a tidier history than the one that occurred.

That is also why evidence here is a SHA rather than a pull request URL. This repository has no
merge commits at all — see [`SCOPE.md`](../SCOPE.md).

`v1.0.0` is immutable history. Everything after it corrects forward.
