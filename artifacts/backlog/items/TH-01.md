## What

Innovation decisions in a repository are governed by standards that a machine can evaluate,
rather than by convention or review habit. A proposal is a parseable artifact, a decision is a
value from a closed enum, and a verdict is computed from catalogued rules.

## Why

The enduring value is that "we considered this properly" stops being an assertion. The theme
outlives any particular pack version, any particular rule, and the current 14 standards.

## Notes

**This backlog records implementation work only.** Innovation proposals are not backlog items.
Proposal liveness, evidence triggers, reconsideration, and decisions are governed by
[`artifacts/innovation-proposals/`](../../innovation-proposals/). A proposal enters the backlog
only after its decision authorizes implementation — a `build` outcome is the single crossing
point; `reject`, `defer` and `explore` stay out.

The full boundary statement, and why no `trackedBy` link exists between the two systems, is in
[`SCOPE.md`](../SCOPE.md). It is worth reading before adding anything here, because the generated
tracker cannot carry that rule — it renders frontmatter only, so this item body and `SCOPE.md`
are the only places it lives.

The theme is marked `COMPLETE` because every leaf beneath it is complete, not because innovation
governance is a finished subject. If the next authorized work opens a new epic here, this reverts
to open — which is the correct behaviour and not a correction.
