## What

Fourteen normative documents, each with scope, numbered requirements, what it adds beyond the
source, its relationship to the others, and an honest implementation section stating what is and
is not mechanically checked. Written in batches of 1–4, 5–9 and 10–14 with a review pause between.

## Why

The normative documents are the irreducible work. The machinery took about a day; these did not.

## Notes

All 27 areas and 17 prohibitions of the original prompt are owned exactly once, plus six additional
prohibitions discovered while writing (A1–A6) and the integrity invariant itself.

Standard 14 is the keystone and the one most likely to be misread. It enumerates the four mechanical
protections of the invariant — a waiver against a non-exemptible rule is rejected rather than
honoured; an attestation never overrides an automated finding; a rule nothing evaluated is reported
unevaluated rather than passing; a policy declaring a rule below its catalog level is itself a
finding — **and their limits**. A sufficiently privileged actor can edit the tests. Claiming
self-protection the system cannot deliver would itself have violated the assurance-honesty
principle the standard exists to hold.

Those four protections later turned out to be the real authority that eight of the twenty stale
vendored comments had been reaching for.
